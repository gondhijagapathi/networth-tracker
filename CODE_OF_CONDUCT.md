# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and respectful to other people
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainer. Reports will be reviewed promptly
and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently
ban any contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate.
